x402 for WordPress: a crypto paywall for your content

NanoTab brings x402 payments to WordPress. Put any post, download, or API endpoint behind a WordPress USDC paywall — readers pay a few cents in stablecoin, you keep 99.9%. No Stripe account, no PCI scope, no chargebacks.

1 · Install the plugin

wp plugin install nanotab-x402 --activate

Or upload the zip via Plugins → Add New. Then go to Settings → NanoTab and paste your USDC receiving address on Base mainnet.

2 · Paywall a post

Wrap premium content in the shortcode:

[nanotab price=0.05]Premium content here[/nanotab]

Visitors see a paywall card: price in USDC, a QR for wallets, and — for AI agents — a machine-readable 402 response on the same URL. One endpoint serves humans and agents.

3 · The 402 flow, in words

  1. A reader (or agent) requests the protected URL. The plugin returns 402 Payment Required with price, merchant wallet, and the USDC-on-Base asset id.
  2. The reader signs a USDC payment intent and resubmits with an X-Payment header.
  3. The NanoTab facilitator verifies the payment, the plugin unlocks the content, and funds settle to your wallet minus a 0.1% fee.

FAQ

How do I add a crypto paywall to WordPress?
Install the NanoTab plugin, set your wallet address, and use the [nanotab] shortcode around premium content. Readers pay USDC — no Stripe needed.
What fees does a WordPress USDC paywall charge?
0.1% per payment. A $1 article costs you a tenth of a cent, versus 33¢ on Stripe.
Do readers need an account or credit card?
No. Any Base-compatible wallet works, and AI agents can pay through the same endpoint programmatically.
