No API keys, no subscriptions, no card minimums. This is the only hands-on builder guide to accept agentic payments; everything else ranking for this keyword is enterprise thought-leadership. What agentic payments are

Agentic payments are purchases made by software: an AI agent with a wallet calls your API, sees a machine-readable price in the 402 response, signs a USDC transfer, and retries with an X-Payment header. A facilitator verifies the payment and proxies the request to your origin. The agent's budget caps, signing, and spend logs are handled by its SDK — you just collect USDC on Base mainnet, settled instantly, keeping 99.9%.

FAQ

What are agentic payments?
Payments made autonomously by AI agents over HTTP 402 (x402): the agent signs USDC and retries — no API keys or subscriptions.
How do I receive agentic payments?
Run npx nanotab buy-init, price an endpoint, return 402 to unpaid callers, and point at gateway.nanotab.dev. Live in ~5 minutes.
What does it cost to accept agentic payments?
Flat 0.1% — you keep 99.9%, settled instantly in USDC on Base. Stripe takes 2.9%+30¢; Adyen charges interchange++ per region.
Do buyers need an account or API key?
No. Agents pay from a USDC wallet (gas sponsored); humans pay from any Base wallet. No accounts or sign-ups either side.
Can I use this alongside Stripe or Adyen?
Yes — keep cards for human checkout, add the 402 path for agent traffic and sub-$1 micropayments where cards lose money.
