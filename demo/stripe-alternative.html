Cheaper than Stripe for micropayments

Stripe is great for $20 checkouts. It is structurally incapable of micropayments: 2.9% + 30¢ means a 5¢ charge costs you 33¢ in fees. NanoTab is the Stripe x402 alternative built for per-call billing — a flat 0.1%, no fixed fee, 1¢ minimum, settled instantly in USDC.

5¢ worked example: pay-per-API-call

Say your API costs 5¢ per call and serves 100,000 calls a month ($5,000 gross):

  • Stripe: impossible — fees ($30,145) exceed revenue. The classic Stripe minimum payment workaround is bundling into monthly invoices, which means credit risk, dunning, and engineering overhead.
  • NanoTab: $5.00 in total fees. Each call returns 402 with a 5¢ price; the caller's agent pays USDC and retries automatically. No invoices, no accounts, no dunning.

How the Stripe-x402-style flow works

import { nanotabFetch } from '@nanotab/agent-sdk';
const res = await nanotabFetch('https://your-api.example.com/v1/score');

On the seller side you protect the endpoint (see the facilitator guide); on the buyer side one function call handles price discovery, signing, and retry. The same pattern works for human checkout with a wallet button.

FAQ

Why can't Stripe do micropayments?
The fixed 30¢ per-transaction fee exceeds the price of anything under ~$1. Micropayments on Stripe lose money on every charge.
What is cheaper than Stripe for micropayments?
NanoTab at flat 0.1% with no fixed fee — a 5¢ payment costs five-thousandths of a cent and settles instantly in USDC on Base.
What is the Stripe minimum payment workaround?
Usually bundling micro-charges into monthly invoices or prepaid wallet top-ups. NanoTab removes the need entirely with true per-call billing over HTTP 402.
